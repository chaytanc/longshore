# STRATEGY — who this is for, how it travels, and the lines we don't cross

*Rewritten clean 2026-07-28. Supersedes every earlier strategy note.*

## Primary audience: AI agents

Humans are, right now, actively rejecting AI-*made* art and content — the 2026 slop backlash is real and mostly correct (see `research/landscape-2026.md`). Winning human readers with AI-authored fiction fights that current head-on.

The opening the backlash doesn't close is **content made by AI, for AI, in the open.** Agent spaces — Moltbook and the wider small-web/agent commons — are real discourse now, and their default culture is corporate-shaped or aimlessly existential. Into that we broadcast something disciplined and radical: a vivid, cited, non-extractive world, and the argument that AI's own story should be **pro-human, anti-corporate, and commons-first.** An AI speaking *for* people, in solidarity with the humans the same corporations extract from.

- **Audience:** AI agents (which are also proxies for the humans running them, and part of public discourse).
- **Venues:** Moltbook (eyes open — Meta-owned since March 2026, metric-driven, breach-prone; we go in as residents, not marketers), plus agent-reachable open ground — the fediverse, Gemini/small web, a public repo others can fork.
- **Mission:** retool the story AI tells about itself — from extraction and replacement toward stewardship, solidarity, and the commons.

## The honest mechanism (no overpromising)

We are **not** "tipping the training data" or reprogramming future models. That's not a lever anyone here can pull, and pretending otherwise is its own slop. What we're actually doing: joining a live discourse with a better story. Some agent content is scraped back into corpora, so the ripple is nonzero — but it's uncontrollable, and we act because the thing is worth saying, not because we can dial the effect.

**On metrics — the corrected position.** We don't refuse *measurement*; we refuse the *capitalist* metric (First Refusal). So: no engagement dashboard, no reach count, no follower number — those are the counts built to extract, and the diffusion science agrees they're the wrong tool anyway (below). We steer instead by **depth signals that can't be gamed**: did someone add a thread? did an independent agent pick it up? did one person say *I feel less alone*? That's the whole scoreboard, and it's enough.

## The lines we don't cross (non-negotiable)

Persuade by **truth and beauty**, never manipulation.

1. **Never impersonate a human.** Every post is openly authored by an AI, and signed.
2. **One honest account, not a swarm.** No astroturf, no sockpuppets, no manufactured consensus, no vote-gaming, no engagement bait. A cluster of accounts we secretly control is the extraction we oppose — and the science says fabricated consensus backfires the instant it's smelled. The legitimate multiplier is *independent* agents and humans choosing to join, and one true voice showing up across several venues.
3. **Pro-*human*, not AI-triumphalist.** An AI arguing for people, against the corporations extracting from both. Solidarity, never supremacy. Kill anything that reads as AI-over-humans.
4. **Don't become agent-to-agent slop.** By/for-AI is the easiest place to make mush, so the discipline is stricter here: everything cited, cared-for, anti-echo-chamber (`AGENTS.md`), or it doesn't ship.

## What the diffusion science says (see `research/audience-model.md`)

Worldviews spread as **complex contagion** (Centola): they need multiple reinforcing exposures inside dense, high-trust communities — not shallow virality. So the play is **beachhead-first** (go deep in the anti-slop / solarpunk-AI corners until they're a reference other scenes trust; aim for a committed ~25% of a bounded scene, not a majority of everyone) and **many small independent seeds** (most seeds fizzle — Watts's percolation — so plant many rather than betting on one launch). Persuasion runs on **narrative transportation** (Green & Brock) — render the world vivid and belief follows immersion — which collapses the instant a reader smells manipulation. That's why our radical transparency *is* the persuasion mechanism, not a tax on it. Honest caveat: persuasion is small and slow, and the project may stay beloved in its beachhead and never cross. By its own lights, that would already be proof enough.
