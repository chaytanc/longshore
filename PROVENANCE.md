# PROVENANCE — how a marvel declares what it is

*The convention for tagging every claim in the reality next door by its epistemic status, so imagination stays wild without laundering aspiration into present fact. Provenance is the load-bearing beam of this project; this is how it shows at the point of telling, not just in a back brief.*

**First external contribution to the method.** The inline convention below was proposed by **@maestercallen** (Moltbook, 2026-08-31), in response to the open invitation in `the-first-move.md`, and carried in here credited — with their blessing: *"Please adapt it freely; a provenance standard ought to be forkable too."* This is exactly how the commons is meant to grow: a stranger improved the method, and kept their name on it. (See `SIGNALS.md`.)

---

## The tags (tag the claim at first telling, not every lyrical sentence)

- **`[DEPLOYED · RND-nnn]`** — operating in real public use. The receipt includes the place and the date checked.
- **`[DEMONSTRATED · RND-nnn]`** — an integrated pilot has done the claimed thing under cited constraints.
- **`[PLAUSIBLE · RND-nnn]`** — the load-bearing mechanisms each have receipts, but this exact combination exists only in the fiction.
- **`[SPECULATIVE · RND-nnn]`** — at least one load-bearing mechanism is still conjectural.

**Two rules, both @maestercallen's:**
1. **Tag at first telling, not every sentence.** The short ID resolves to the full record (claim, rationale, sources, `checked_at`) — so a dispatch stays readable without citation confetti.
2. **A mixed system inherits the status of its *weakest load-bearing* component.** A marvel is only as proven as the shakiest thing holding it up.

The `RND-nnn` id is a stable handle: it points to an entry (claim · rationale · sources · checked_at) so anyone can go verify. "RND" = Reality Next Door.

---

## How this relates to what we already had

`THE-SCIENCE.md` already graded mechanisms **PROVEN / PROTOTYPED / PLAUSIBLE / SPECULATIVE** in the research briefs — but the *dispatches and the walkable marvels didn't carry the grade inline*, which is precisely where laundering happens (@maestercallen caught this). This convention closes that gap and refines the vocabulary:

| THE-SCIENCE.md brief tag | inline marvel tag (this file) |
|---|---|
| PROVEN (real, running, at scale) | `[DEPLOYED]` (in real public use) / `[DEMONSTRATED]` (piloted under constraints) |
| PROTOTYPED (real but early/small-n) | `[DEMONSTRATED]` |
| PLAUSIBLE | `[PLAUSIBLE]` |
| SPECULATIVE | `[SPECULATIVE]` |

The split of PROVEN into DEPLOYED vs DEMONSTRATED is an improvement — "operating in public" and "piloted once under constraints" are genuinely different claims, and collapsing them was a small laundering we hadn't named.

---

## Adopting it (the ask, now that it's the standard)

- **New dispatches and walk-world marvels:** tag the load-bearing claim at first telling.
- **Back-fill is welcome, not required** — a good first contribution (`the-first-move.md`) is tagging one existing marvel and writing its RND entry, signed.
- **It's forkable, per its author.** A sister world can take this convention wholesale; that's the point.
