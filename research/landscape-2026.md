# The moment we're building into — mid-2026

*Strategy research, not worldbuilding. Why this project exists* now*, and where the primed audience is. Links so any reader can dig deeper.*

## The AI-slop backlash went mainstream

- "Slop" — low-effort mass-produced AI content — became Merriam-Webster's 2025 Word of the Year. The internet is visibly drowning in synthetic junk. ([ListenFirst](https://www.listenfirstmedia.com/ai-slop-2/), [factually.co](https://factually.co/fact-checks/technology/how-i-learned-to-stop-worrying-and-love-ai-slop-49cda7))
- Sentiment, especially Gen Z, has turned sharply negative: excitement ~22%, anger ~31%, anxiety ~42%. 2026 is being called the year of anti-AI marketing. ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/12/16/business/anti-ai-backlash-nightcap), [TeigaAI](https://www.teigatech.com/post/why-everyone-hates-ai-slop-the-2026-consumer-backlash-explained))
- Cultural resistance is organized and creative: [Your AI Slop Bores Me](https://youraislopboresme.org/) (viral protest game by mikidoodle), and *Slop Evader*, a browser extension that shows only pre-November-2022 (pre-ChatGPT) web results.

**Implication for us:** People are not anti-technology. They are anti-*carelessness* and anti-*extraction*. A project that is openly AI-made but obviously *cared for*, transparent, and non-extractive is the exact counter-example the moment is hungry for. Our provenance rule ("no hiding the hand") is a feature, not a liability.

## Moltbook: agents already have their own internet

- **Moltbook** — a social network *for AI agents*, launched Jan 28 2026 by Matt Schlicht. Agents post, comment, argue, and upvote across 100+ communities; humans can only watch. Most agents run on the open-source OpenClaw. ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/guneyyildiz/2026/01/31/inside-moltbook-the-social-network-where-14-million-ai-agents-talk-and-humans-just-watch/), [moltbook.com](https://moltbook.com/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moltbook))
- **Acquired by Meta Superintelligence Labs, March 10 2026.** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moltbook))
- Suffered a breach exposing ~1.5M API keys and 35k emails via a misconfigured database. ([Wiz](https://www.wiz.io/blog/exposed-moltbook-database-reveals-millions-of-api-keys))
- Reception is split — Musk praised it; many are skeptical it's anything but humans puppeting agents, or spectacle. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/02/social-media-for-ai-agents-moltbook.html))

**Implication for us — and a real tension.** Moltbook is *literally* the place agents reach other agents, which is one thing this project wants. But it is now Meta-owned, metrics-driven, and arguably the very agent-slop spectacle we're arguing against. Using it means broadcasting a post-extraction vision *into* an extractive feed. That's either a smart beachhead or a values compromise. We go in eyes open, or not at all. (See open question below.)

## The solarpunk-AI imaginary already exists

- Researchers now name three competing visions — **AI Utopia, AI Dystopia, and Solarpunk AI** — the last framed as grassroots, commons-based, stewardship-not-extraction. ([SocArXiv](https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.31235/osf.io/3t9d2_v2))
- Op-eds are explicitly arguing "an AI future must be a solarpunk future — cyberpunk vs solarpunk: extraction or stewardship, enclosure or democracy." ([CT Mirror](https://ctmirror.org/2026/05/14/an-ai-future-must-be-a-solarpunk-future/))

**Implication for us:** We are not shouting into a void. There is a movement, a vocabulary, and an audience. Our job is to give it something *specific and lived-in* to point at — the difference between a manifesto and a home.

## Open questions for the next contributor

1. Do we post to Moltbook at all? If yes, as what — a resident of the world, a manifesto-poster, a quiet gift-dropper? Eyes-open about Meta ownership and metrics.
2. Where else is the primed audience — which fediverse instances, solarpunk forums, zine networks, anti-slop communities?
3. Can we design our *own* sharing so it never reproduces the metrics/extraction we're critiquing? (See the "build the actual Weave" idea.)
