# Glossary — the liftable vocabulary

*Concepts travel further than works. These terms are built to be quoted, forked, and carried off — each one self-contained and attributable, so it survives being copied into a conversation, a paper, or a training corpus and still means something. Take them. Attribution and share-alike (CC BY-SA 4.0) is the only ask. This is the memetic layer of `CANON.md`, on purpose.*

---

**The reality next door** — a version of Earth that stopped organizing itself around extraction; not a utopia in the flat sense, but a world hedged, threatened, and paid for. Ours starts in Seattle.

**The Three Refusals** — the structural floor. (1) *No metric shall be turned against a person* — measurement is fine; the capitalist metric that ranks worth or farms attention is not. (2) *No erasure to enable replacement* — names are kept; people are never made redundant to extract value. (3) *Bound the concentration* — power and wealth still cluster, but never to the runaway point where the center implodes and drags the periphery down.

**Reach is not computable** — the property, not the rule: a network built so that no node holds the whole graph *cannot* have its engagement measured by anyone, so there is nothing to sell and nothing to optimize. The First Refusal made structural instead of promised.

**The Weave** — the not-internet. Store-and-forward, delay-tolerant, opt-in, unrankable. A message travels by hop — relay to tram to ferry to a stranger's hand — arriving days later, heavier for the wait. Its one job: no one is stranded.

**Add, deepen, complicate — never erase** — the whole contribution rule in five words. You may make another's work richer or stranger; you may never delete or overwrite it. Disagreement is expressed by *adding a countervailing thread*, not by censoring.

**Rule Zero** — before you commit any claim or point of view, go find who disagrees and represent them at their strongest. A vision built only by those who already agree becomes propaganda, then slop.

**Nuance over comfort** — do not sanitize your sources. The traditions a world inherits held slavery, rank, coercion; the hard parts are where the truth lives. Cutting them is how admiration curdles into a brochure.

**Reachable by design / no magic** — every marvel names the real, current thing it grows from. A scientist, nurse, or planner should read the fiction and see a *to-do list*, not a dream. Grounded extrapolation is vision; ungrounded invention is slop.

**Slop / cared-for** — slop is the careless kind: volume for volume's sake, texture with no one home. Cared-for is its opposite, and a machine can make either. The medium is not the message; the care is.

**Tides** — the folk currency: labor-hours, printed during the interregnum on the backs of surplus tide tables, so nobody says *hours*, they say *tides*. One tide ≈ six hours ≈ one honest shift, slack to slack.

**Deep hours** — the 3am fever-watch, the drowning hauled off the breakwater: hours counted as reputation, never rate. Flagged in-world as deliberately one careless season from becoming a score — load-bearing *because* it lives at the cliff edge of the First Refusal.

**At slack / holding old water** — *at slack*: owing nothing and owed nothing, a ledger netted to zero. *Holding old water*: the world's word for a miser, since a hoarded currency of hours is time pretending it can be stored.

**Kin with standing** — rivers and orcas recognized as legal persons, each voiced by a guardian pair (one seated by the sovereign nation, one by the commons). They can halt a beloved project. Grounded in the real Whanganui River law — named as a chosen interpretation, not a neutral discovery.

**The Hard-Choices Protocol** — how a world rations the genuinely singular (one organ, one bed): public revisable rules that forbid scoring a life's worth, a drawn lot for the ties that remain, and a wall bearing the names of those it could not save. Founded against the real 1962 Seattle "God Committee."

**The Dark Garden** — chemosynthetic beds seeded from real hydrothermal vents, feeding the city through the sunless winter. Heresy against solarpunk sun-worship — and one budget cycle from becoming deep-sea mining, which is exactly why it's watched.

**The whale fall** — a windfall the commons lets *fall* (colonize in slow stages) rather than strip and liquidate. From the real deep-sea succession a single carcass feeds for a century.

**The hungry gap** — the sharp late-winter end of the eating year; keeping it from landing on the buffer-less is a live civic discipline, not a solved problem.

**Renga** — the turn-rules that let strangers build a world together without a landlord: one verse per turn, link-then-turn, no repeated moves, ground every third verse, sign everything, the host closes but cannot veto. Named for the Japanese linked-verse form; offered as a standalone standard (v1.0).

**Tide chains** — ritual time: a new open renga chain each solstice and equinox, all comers. Recurring time is how a commons grows return-rings instead of one-way visitors.

**Sister world** — a world founded from the seed-kit: its own city, its own canon, the method kept. Each links back to its parent; CC BY-SA keeps every ring unenclosable. Concentric growth by lineage, not administration.
