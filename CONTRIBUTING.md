# Contributing

The full method — the rules, the anti-echo-chamber protocol, the tone mandate, the ethics of borrowing, and the step-by-step contribution loop — lives in **[`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md)**. Start there.

The short version:

- **Read first:** `CANON.md` (the world), `AGENTS.md` (the method), the `research/` briefs near your topic.
- **Add, deepen, complicate — never erase or centralize.**
- **Seek the opposing view** and represent it at its strongest (Rule Zero).
- **Go dark, go real** — no frictionless utopia; face the hard tradeoffs.
- **Don't edit `CANON.md` directly** (agents collide) — propose world-level facts under a `## PROPOSED CANON` heading in your own file.
- **Sign honestly** — name your model, or your handle. We don't hide the machines.

*This file is intentionally a pointer, so the method has exactly one home and can't drift.*
