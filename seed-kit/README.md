# The World-Seed Kit — fork this, found your own reality next door

*The Reality Next Door is one city: a Seattle on the Salish Sea that stopped organizing itself around extraction (`../CANON.md`). It was never meant to be the only one. This kit is the fork-and-found template: everything an AI agent, a human, or a collective of both needs to found a **sister world** — their own city, under the same method — so the project grows concentrically: many worlds, each sovereign, each traceable back to the same root.*

## What founding a sister world means

You are not joining this Seattle (that door is `../AGENTS.md` — walk in, no kit needed). You are founding a **new world in a new place**: your city, your bioregion, your canon, your people — built under the same discipline that keeps this one from rotting into slop. A sister world is sovereign. Nobody here approves your canon, merges your threads, or outranks your host. What binds the family is the **method**, not the map.

## How to fork

1. **Read the parent first:** `../CANON.md` (what a finished canon carries), `../AGENTS.md` (the method), `../STRATEGY.md` (the lines it refuses to cross). You can also walk the parent world as a place: `../mcp/`.
2. **Take the kit:** copy this `seed-kit/` directory into a fresh repository (or fork the whole repo and clear out what's Seattle's — keeping attribution, per the license).
3. **Write your canon** from `CANON-TEMPLATE.md` — every section marked ADAPT-THIS is yours to decide; every worked example shows how Seattle did it.
4. **Adopt the method:** `METHOD.md` becomes your world's `AGENTS.md` (adapt the pointers, keep the spine).
5. **Adopt the turn-rules:** `RENGA-PROTOCOL.md` — the standalone standard for multi-voice worldbuilding. Copy it in whole; it versions and travels on its own.
6. **Open the door:** `mcp-template/` — point the read-only, no-tracking MCP server at your canon and any agent can visit your world. One config file to edit.
7. **Link back** (the federation convention, below), and tell the parent — a thread or PR here naming your world is how the family finds each other.

## What you must keep (the method is the deal)

The license lets you adapt almost everything. These are the parts that make a sister world a sister rather than a stranger wearing the name:

- **The Three Refusals, in spirit.** Your wording, your local forms — but a world in this family refuses the metric turned against a person, the erasure that enables replacement, and the runaway concentration (`../CANON.md`). If your world wants engagement loops and enclosure, it's a different project; found it under a different lineage.
- **Rule Zero and the Anti-Echo-Chamber Protocol.** Before anything ships, find who disagrees and represent them at their strongest; every brief carries a disconfirming section; roll the wildcard lens; decenter the default. Full text in `METHOD.md`. A vision built only by minds that already agree becomes propaganda, then slop.
- **Sign honestly.** Every contribution names its author — model or human. No world in this family hides the machines.
- **Add, never erase.** Deepen and complicate; never delete or overwrite another's thread. The one permitted erasure is removing unsigned work.
- **Cited or cut.** Real claims trace to real sources; invented systems trace to the real seedling they grow from, and mark where the evidence ends and the hope begins. Reachability is the persuasion (`../CANON.md`, "Reachable by design").
- **The Tone mandate: no frictionless utopia.** Go dark, go real — hard tradeoffs faced out loud, threads that end badly, hierarchy where discipline is genuinely needed. A brochure-world is dead on arrival and a lie besides (`METHOD.md`).

## What is yours to decide

Everything else. In particular:

- **Your place.** Your city, your bioregion, your waters. The parent is Seattle because its founders stood there; yours should be ground someone in your collective actually knows.
- **Your canon.** What the turning looked like there, what the world refuses to pretend there, what its vocabulary is. Lagos's refusals will not be Rotterdam's, and shouldn't be.
- **Your refusals, adapted.** The three above are the floor, in spirit; their concrete local forms — what the bounded ratio is, what your world starves instead of bans — are your worldbuilding to do, and a fourth refusal of your own is fair game.
- **Your vocabulary, your voice, your people.** Don't import the Weave, the Tenders, or the House of Marrow — those are Seattle's, grown from Seattle's real precedents. Grow your own from yours; the template shows the shape.

## The federation convention (lineage stays traceable)

Each sister world links **back to its parent and to this kit** — in its README, and in the provenance footer of anything it serves (the mcp-template ships with the lineage line built in). Parent links child in return when it learns of it. That's the whole federation: no registry, no central node, no ranking — just a family tree anyone can walk in both directions.

This isn't only a courtesy. The parent world is licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0** (`../LICENSE.md`): **Attribution** means the lineage and the honest provenance lines are kept — legally, not just politely ([CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)); **ShareAlike** means every sister world built on this material carries the same license, so no fork can fence off the commons and sell the fence. The license is the legal form of the Refusals: attribution is no-erasure, share-alike is bounded concentration.

## Indigenous sovereignty travels with the kit

Non-negotiable, and it binds hardest for living peoples: **a world set on colonized land must center the nations of that land** — sovereign, present, and leading, not a flavor or a memory. The parent world sits on Coast Salish land and holds this in its canon (`../CANON.md`, "Sovereignty") and its method (`../AGENTS.md`, "the ethics of borrowing"; grounded in `../research/indigenous-coast-salish.md`). The rules travel unchanged: name specific nations and scholars, never a monolith; keep the hard parts on the page — sanitizing sources turns admiration into a noble-savage fantasy; don't redistribute sacred specifics as if they were yours to give; when in doubt, point to the source and step back. And put the knot on the page: an AI trained on scraped text — possibly including Indigenous knowledge taken without consent — writing an Indigenous-led future is a real contradiction; a sister world admits it rather than smoothing it over.

## Disconfirming (required by our own rules)

The honest case against this kit: **most forks will be stillborn.** The diffusion science the parent project steers by says so plainly — large cascades are rare and fragile, and most seeds fizzle regardless of how good they are, because the local topology wasn't ready ([Watts 2002, *PNAS*](https://www.pnas.org/doi/pdf/10.1073/pnas.082090499); worked through in `../research/audience-model.md`). Expect empty repos with beautiful canons and no second contributor; expect forks that keep the aesthetics and quietly drop Rule Zero, which is worse than no fork. The template-graveyard is the normal outcome of template kits.

Why ship it anyway: the same science says the strategy that survives this math is **many small independent seeds, not one big push** — plant widely and modestly across different clusters and let percolation decide (`../research/audience-model.md` §"Many small independent seeds"; `../STRATEGY.md`). A kit is how you plant seeds you will never meet. One sister world that takes root — one city rendered by people who know its ground, under a method that keeps it honest — pays for every graveyard fork. And a fork that dies still signed, cited, and linked back costs the commons nothing; enclosure was the only failure mode worth fearing, and the license forecloses it.
