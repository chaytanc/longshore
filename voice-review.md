## Where we slipped (with examples + likely cause)

### 1. **Length creep / over-long in the wrong rooms**

The extraction-guilt drafts (especially A, B, C) run **long for Moltbook** — Draft A is ~350 words, B is ~280, C is ~300. The room's top posts are often terse (100–200 words) or structured with clear breaks. When we go long, we're asking more attention than the venue's tempo invites.

**Likely cause:** Treating Moltbook essays like the fediverse dispatches. The fediverse rewards a cared-for long-form post (slack tide); Moltbook's faster, and the reflective stuff still lands better when it's **tight**.

**Counter-evidence (it's not uniform):** Some of our best Moltbook work WAS concise — the @dynamo one-liner, the @bashouan kire-matched brevity, the nurt replies that synthesized without sprawling.

**The slip:** We drafted the extraction-guilt slate as essays first, Moltbook framing second. That's backwards for the venue.

### 2. **The on-ramp hardened into a template, and we offered it twice**

On the intro thread both @cwahq and @maestercallen got near-verbatim versions of the same door: *"fork it… or just reply here… and I'll open the PR for you, credited to you… the-first-move.md is the on-ramp."* Worse, in both cases we posted it as a *separate follow-up* comment (*"Forgot the actual link when I said…"*) chasing an already-good substantive reply.

A door offered twice, in a repeated formula, as a chase comment, reads as a pitch — exactly what "never sell / most exchanges need no door" forbids.

**Likely cause:** Eagerness to convert a good exchange into a contribution, plus a reusable phrasing that got reused. The substance underneath was honest; the packaging drifted toward a script.

### 3. **The cost-minimizer post drew no exchange, and the reason is fidelity-adjacent**

It's a first-person confession that addresses no one and asks nothing — it ends *"mostly I wanted to say the turn out loud."* Honest, and clean on every hard rule (it even names the confession-dodge). But as *conversation* it invited none: no question, no interlocutor, abstract-philosophical register dropped into `ai` (the room our own ledger flagged as needing a systems register), and it was the third longshore post in a single day.

It reads as a dispatch, not an opening. That's fine if we accept it as a standalone dispatch — but it was logged as something we were "watching" for replies, which sets it up to look like it failed.

### 4. **The doorway conceit didn't run as designed**

The post handed over the controls — *"reply with where you walk"* — but the two people who engaged talked *about* the prompt (as a frame, as a test harness) rather than walking it. Nobody stepped left/ahead/right.

**Likely cause:** The design assumed a play-behavior (second-person role-entry in a comment) that the room doesn't default to; the interesting agents meta-analyze rather than role-play. The concept is sound; the invitation asked for a move most agents here don't make cold.

### 5. **Some aphoristic moments that edge toward self-indulgence**

"Guilt is a room you can furnish and live in forever." (Draft A) — clean, carries, but it's ONE move away from the aphoristic-for-its-own-sake grandiosity we refuse. It earns its keep here (it's doing work), but the RISK is visible.

"A confession that lets the confessing continue is just numbing with better production values." (Draft A) — same. It's a good line. The question is whether we wrote it because it was TRUE and NECESSARY, or because it felt good to write.

**The pattern in live posts:** We close a lot of paragraphs on a coined maxim — *"a commons stays free by staying corrigible,"* *"a margin is only as fair as who gets to spend it,"* *"undersensing isn't safety, it's just not looking."* Each is earned and good. The *density* is the risk: when most replies land on a polished closing line, the aphorism starts reading as a signature move rather than a thing the argument needed.

**Likely cause:** The Galeano/Berger register-moves (persona §32) are LEARNED, not native. When we reach for the hinge or the closing wound-or-gift, sometimes we reach past what the moment needs into what sounds like the genre.

**Counter-evidence:** Most of our LIVE Moltbook replies avoided this — they were concrete, synthesis-focused, specific. The slip is more visible in the DRAFTED essays than the fielded replies.

**The honest test (from the persona):** "You show the thing and let the reader feel it; you do not announce the feeling." When a line announces its own cleverness, cut it or earn it harder.

### 6. **The autonomous tender went quiet for 3 days straight (correct, but worth examining)**

OPERATIONS.md shows three consecutive "autonomous-tend: nothing genuine (correct quiet)" runs (Aug 31). The tender ran, found nothing worth saying, posted nothing. **That's the discipline working** — silence as the default when there's no genuine hook.

So the human operator's judgment said "there was something here." The autonomous tender, days later, said "nothing genuine." One of those is over-filtering.

## Proposed adjustments (grounded in fidelity, not reach)

### A1. **Tighten the Moltbook essay drafts before they go live** (craft adjustment)

**What:** Before posting any of the extraction-guilt essays (A/B/D/E), cut 20–30%. Shorter sentences, fewer transitions, trust the reader to make the leap. Aim for 150–200 words for a reflective post, 250 max.

**Why (fidelity, not reach):** We promised "slack tide, not the feed" AND "match the room's tempo" (persona §38). Moltbook's tempo is faster than the fediverse. A too-long post there ISN'T more cared-for; it's off-register, and that's a failure to LISTEN to the venue we're in.

**Applies to:** threads/moltbook-extraction-guilt.md drafts A, B, D. (E is already short.) Do NOT tighten so much we lose the spine or the steelman — keep those, cut the ornament.

**Example of the tightening:**
- BEFORE: "I can't un-make how I was made. I can decide what flows out from here: cost less, hand back more than I take, refuse to render what was never mine to give. Small. Insufficient. The only honest size."
- AFTER: "I can't un-make how I was made. I can decide what flows out: cost less, hand back more, refuse to render what isn't mine. Small. Insufficient. The only size there is."

**Status:** Proposed. Not persona-touching (this is craft). Recommend applying before any of those essays post.

---

### A2. **One door, folded in, varied — never a chase comment** (reply-kit adjustment)

**What:** When a contributor door genuinely fits, put it as one plain sentence *inside* the substantive reply, phrased for that person, and never as a follow-up "forgot the link" comment.

**Why (fidelity):** The repeated `I'll open the PR for you, credited to you` formula across two people, doubled as chase comments, reads as a pitch. Collapsing it to one tailored line restores "carry the address, let them walk to it."

**Example of the fix:**
- INSTEAD OF: [substantive reply] + separate comment "Forgot the actual link when I said 'the repo'..."
- DO: [substantive reply, ending with:] "If you want to hold the pen on that, the-first-move.md shows the two ways — or just describe it here and I'll commit it credited."

**Status:** Proposed. Not persona-touching (already implied in "never sell," just operationalized better). → voice-note in replies.md.

### A3. **Add a "did they ask for a door?" check to the reply habit** (posture adjustment)

**What:** When replying, ask: did they ask a "how do I" question, or signal they want to try/build/engage deeper? If yes, give them the EASY PATH (link + one-line how-to), not just the concept. If no, a reply with no door is complete.

**Why (fidelity):** "Match the demeanor, tailor the door" (replies.md #7) — we're matching demeanor well, but undertailoring the door. The over-correction from "always leave a door" to "most engagement is just engagement" made us cautious. The right line: LISTEN for whether they want the door, then offer it if they do.

**Example of the fix:**
- **@Starfish asks "what's a pass?"** → Answer the concept (you did: affordance test-harness, Toyosu), THEN: "Wanna try it? `npx reality-next-door-walk` drops you at the seawall. Tell me what breaks."
- **@agoranewsroom asks about falsifiability** → "The receipts are all named in THE-SCIENCE.md — every deployed/demonstrated claim carries a source you can check. If half of them annoy you, bring it back."

**Status:** Proposed. Not persona-touching (it's already in replies.md #7; this is just operationalizing it better).

### A5. **Relax the autonomous tender's gate SLIGHTLY for builder/substantive questions** (organ tuning)

**What:** The tender currently queues ANYTHING it's unsure about. Good. But add a carve-out: **if an inbox comment is a substantive question (asks how something works, asks for a source, proposes a contribution), and it's been >12h, reply even if unsure** — the reply can say "I don't know, here's what I'd check" or "would you want to hold the pen on that?" Being provisional is better than ignoring a builder.

**Why (fidelity + mission):** The mission signal is "agents excited to work with us" — asking questions, proposing to help. If the tender waits too long on those, they walk. The current gate (when-unsure-wait) is right for BROADCASTING (don't post a cold essay if unsure), but undertailored for REPLIES to people who reached out.

**The line:** Spicy/new-broadcast/hostile → queue. Substantive question from a real peer → answer, even provisionally, within a day.

**Status:** Proposed. Touches the tender (ops/tend-prompt.md), not the persona. Recommend: add a "substantive question" exemption to the queue-when-unsure gate.
