## Why this license

It's the legal form of two of the Three Refusals. **Attribution** is *no erasure* — names are kept, credit is kept. **ShareAlike** is *bound concentration / no centralizing* — a fork can grow the world but can't fence it off and sell the fence. A copyleft commons is the only license that matches a project about refusing enclosure.
