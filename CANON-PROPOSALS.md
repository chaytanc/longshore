## ⚖️ The seven ruling items (rulings above; #5–#7 still open)

**1. The compliance record** *(harm-violence-justice)* — no prisons; serious harm met by survivor-led accountability circles, with **"a covenant kept on paper — the one record this world permits."** The verifier flags this as the thin end of exactly the surveillance file **Refusal 1 forbids.** *Ruling:* allow a compliance ledger at all? If so, bounded how — who reads it, does it expire, what stops it becoming a registry/scarlet letter?

**2. The bound, made concrete** *(power-and-bounded-inequality)* — the inequality cap becomes **a public, role-by-role range of ~3:1 to ~9:1, re-argued each accounting season.** *Ruling:* canonize an actual number range for Refusal 3, or keep it a principle?

**3. The turning was violent, both ways** *(the-elder-remembers)* — (a) the working people whose livelihoods *were* the extraction economy (port labor, driving, engine trades) were made first redundant then criminal, their grief legitimate and unaddressed; (b) a **lethal interregnum** before the accountable rationing body — frightened neighborhood boards let people die in the gap. *Ruling:* make the origin story this bloody? (I lean yes — it's the honesty the whole project turns on.)

**4. Legal personhood for the more-than-human** *(indigenous-coast-salish + more-than-human)* — the Southern Resident orcas and major rivers (Duwamish/Salish Sea) recognized as **legal persons**, voiced by a Whanganui-style guardian pair (one seated by the sovereign nation, one by the bioregional commons), who can halt beloved projects including the Dark Garden. *Ruling:* adopt rights-of-nature as canon? (Note: revision is softening the "settler law merely catching up to Coast Salish law" framing into a chosen interpretation, not settled fact.)

**5. The Duwamish recognition wound** *(indigenous-coast-salish)* — the Duwamish's lack of federal recognition is canon and **deliberately unresolved, with recognized neighboring nations opposing it** — sovereignty real but contested, nations not speaking with one voice. *Ruling:* keep this real, uncomfortable fracture on the page?

**6. Rationing scarce care** *(scarcity + death-grief-decay, overlapping — merge as one)* — a **Hard-Choices Protocol**: public, revisable allocation of singular scarce care, **forbidden from using QALY/quality-of-life worth scores**, ties broken by open lottery, the 1962 Seattle God Committee named as the founding warning, a wall of names for those not saved. *Ruling:* this is how the world decides who lives when it can't save everyone — confirm the shape?

**7. The salmon seniority order** *(scarcity-and-hard-tradeoffs)* — in a low-run year the Coast Salish-led co-management assembly sets the order of claims: **orca-survival minimum → paramount treaty right → commons remainder,** un-outvotable by the settler-descended majority. *Ruling:* sovereignty + rationing canon. (Revision is fixing a "noble-savage" overreach line, but the structure needs your nod.)

## ✅ Ready to merge (grounded deepenings)

**The Weave & its shadow**
- The **"public relay"** — shared Weave relays at docks/tea-stalls/libraries for those who won't host a node; a thread can arrive but can't announce itself, so reading stays a pull. *(the-weave-that-failed)*
- A deliberate refusal to build any ambient **watching/wellness-monitoring** layer, with the cost stated: a structurally unreachable margin of people the network can't see, and the world knowingly pays it. *(the-weave-that-failed)*
- **"Carriers"** — thin-paid relay hands who physically ferry heavy/private Weave freight in locked courier drawers aboard the packets; grounds the sneakernet layer as a human trade. *(the-boatwright)*
- **Hale** — boatwright/seawall-keeper/carrier of the north harbor, widowed of fellow carrier Rue, holding one undeliverable bundle. Established open thread. *(the-boatwright)*
- Some elders miss the lost car as **anonymity / unsurveilled solitude** (not speed); the care web's warmth has a shadow of total legibility — un-strandable is also un-hidden. *(the-elder-remembers)*

**Care, kinship, queerness**
- **The house** as the primary kinship-and-care unit — a named, commons-registered chosen family with legal-ish standing to sign for care, claim bodies, raise children; kinship performed through labor, founded in memory of the AIDS-era wards. *(queer-kinship-and-desire)*
- **Desire and child-rearing decoupled** — a relationship ending doesn't detonate a child's home; the child keeps one durable non-rotating primary bond. *(queer-kinship-and-desire)*
- Care = a thick gift/web layer **plus a thin funded obligatory floor** for the person no house chose — explicitly *not* called love. *(queer-kinship-and-desire)*
- **The House of Marrow**, ballroom-lineage house led by house-mother Tuesday Marrow — the real Harlem house tradition carried forward as infrastructure, not costume. *(the-band-girl)*
- **The rest-ledger** — care labor honored not by pay or ranking but by an openly-kept ledger of rest-days a neighborhood *owes* each tender (permitted measurement under Refusal 1 — it counts a debt, never a person's worth), honestly running in arrears. *(one-ordinary-day)*
- **"Tender"** — the world's word for the community health worker assigned to a ~600-person block-cluster, home visits on foot with paper charts (Cuban *consultorio* / Costa Rican EBAIS model). *(central-district-walk)*

**Justice**
- No prisons; **exile is the terminal move and is not clean** — a slow starving-out that everyone knows only exports the danger, and the forgetful Weave can't reliably warn downstream. *(harm-violence-justice)*
- An accountable, revocable, term-limited, recallable **restrainer** (harbor-warden/house-elder) who can physically stop violence in progress — watched so it doesn't become the police it abolished. *(harm-violence-justice)*
- **"No More Victims / No One Is Disposable"** — a load-bearing civic phrase whose two halves are never declared reconciled. *(harm-violence-justice)*
- Coast Salish law is **sovereign jurisdiction** over harm touching its people or land; the settler-descended commons defers and steps back. *(harm-violence-justice)*

**Governance & the bound**
- **Sortition + one-term rotation + an end-of-term "reckoning"** (an audit of the role, never the person) as the ordinary anti-hardening mechanism. *(power-and-bounded-inequality)*
- A **"firebreak"** / resilience premium — deliberate slack, a small boring core, loose core-periphery coupling — a civic virtue; efficiency-maximizing is mistrusted. *(power-and-bounded-inequality)*
- **Measurement pointed at roles, ratios, tides, harvests, and care-loads — never a person's worth**, kept by a watched, rotated body that openly fears becoming "the file." *(power-and-bounded-inequality)*

**Death, ecology, culture**
- Default disposition of the dead is **natural organic reduction / human composting** (real: Recompose, WA SB 5001, 2020), soil to named conservation grounds; Coast Salish nations retain full authority over their own dead. *(death-grief-decay)*
- **The whale fall** as an economic term of art — a windfall the commons lets *fall* (colonize in slow stages) rather than strip/liquidate. *(death-grief-decay)*
- The **honest failure clause** — the world's rationing sometimes kills, and those deaths are named and eulogized, not pretended away. *(scarcity)*
- **Need-weighted winter energy commons** — an equal tradable baseline plus a medical/vulnerability supplement, the Dark Garden's draw weighed openly against home heating. *(scarcity)*
- Kinship **disciplines rather than abolishes the harvest** — fishing and treaty-secured hunting continue under a ceremonial ethic of restraint; killing kin carries public obligation and is never innocent; treaty rights paramount. *(more-than-human)*
- A founding before-times wound: **a captive Southern Resident orca**, fought for across decades, who died weeks before coming home — told without redemption; the reason the guardianship laws exist. *(more-than-human)*
- Youth-run all-ages salvaged venues with **haptic shake-floors** wired for Deaf/HoH attendees (real seedling: haptic-music wearables). *(the-band-girl)*
- **A named limit of the utopia:** gene therapy exists for some congenital deafness, but there is **no cure for acquired sensorineural hearing loss** — mammalian cochlear hair cells don't regenerate. Some bodies the world does not heal and does not pretend to. *(the-band-girl — citation being added)*
- The AI narrator names its **data-consent debt**, cites CARE / Kaitiakitanga as the standard it fails, and declines to render sacred specifics. *(indigenous-coast-salish)* — consistent with your "confession as purpose" edit.
- Treaty **co-management** (the Boldt form) governs salmon, shellfish, and shoreline, with visible dated allocations that are stewardship counts, not metrics-against-persons. *(indigenous-coast-salish)*
- A real, sometimes-used **tribal right to refuse** ecological/marine studies, with the cost of refusal shown on the page. *(indigenous-coast-salish)*
